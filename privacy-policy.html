Unit Converter

Chrome Extension · Privacy Policy
Last updated October 19, 2026

Unit Converter is built to do one thing — convert values between units — without collecting anything about you or your browsing.

The short version: Unit Converter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or identifiable information. There are no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third parties involved.

Permissions we use, and why

contextMenus

Adds a "Convert" option to the right-click menu when you've selected text on a page, so you can jump straight to a conversion without opening the popup first.

storage

Used only for chrome.storage.session, a temporary, in-memory store built into Chrome. When you choose a conversion from the right-click menu, the selected text and target units are held here just long enough to hand off to the popup, then cleared immediately. This data is never written to disk, never synced to any account, and is always wiped when the browser closes.

What happens to selected text

When you right-click on selected text and choose a conversion, Chrome passes that text to the extension. Unit Converter reads it only to extract a number, or to spell it out phonetically, and discards it immediately after. It is never transmitted anywhere — the extension makes no network requests of any kind, to us or anyone else.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We don't use cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We don't serve ads.
  • We don't sell or share data of any kind, because none is collected.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

Children's privacy

Unit Converter doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the extension adds a feature that touches data in a new way — this page will be updated and the date above will change to reflect it.

Questions about this extension can be sent through its support page on the Chrome Web Store listing.
